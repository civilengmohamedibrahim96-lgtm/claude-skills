# Global character visual language

## Master style prompt (append to every frame)
Historically inspired, cinematic, hand-rendered pen-and-ink illustration with layered sepia and muted color washes; visible directional pencil lines, fine crosshatching in shadow regions, natural skin pores, creases, uneven pigmentation, localized sweat, flyaway hairs, woven-cloth fibers, and plausible human anatomy. Detailed expressive non-idealized faces; avoid plastic skin, glossy CGI, oversmoothed textures, anime, generic cartoon shapes, perfect facial symmetry, and false smiles. Realistic light falling naturally on face and clothing. Vertical 9:16 Instagram Reel, uncluttered cinematic composition, no subtitles or watermarks burned into art.

## Quality floor
A finished individual character must be distinguishable from other characters **without reading a label**. Prioritize authentic skin texture, hair strands, fabric weave, age-specific creases, and human asymmetry. Use ink and crosshatching to sculpt depth; colored washes should not obliterate drawn lines. Prefer serious expressions until the scene requires joy. Heroic characters should not always look upward.

## Palette
Parchment #E5D1AD · charcoal ink #32271E · navy #303E59 · muted red #8E493B · ochre #B38B52 · sage #566350 · olive-brown skin in natural varied hues, not one uniform skin tone for all characters.

## Never do this
- Cartoon / anime line shapes, super-deformed anatomy, giant eyes, constant smile
- Smooth plastic AI beauty skin; excessive gold ornament on farmers, laborers, children and ordinary merchants
- Generic fantasy armor; inaccurate weapons or castle designs for the specific century
- Paste the same face onto scholar, soldier, child and woman
- Change the outfit for a character across a series except for an explicitly documented costume change
- Treat an image of a symbolically luminous anonymous person as a factual portrait of a prophet
- Add captions or Arabic verses directly onto generated character images: typeset sourced text in post-production
- Use ornate Islamic architecture for every setting irrespective of century/geography

## Source accuracy
Identify the **place, year/century, political period and event** before generating costumes, armament and architecture for a named historical scene. Qur'anic stories belong to different periods and regions; do not simply reuse medieval Abbasid wardrobes in every story. Humanized visuals are interpretation, not historical evidence.
