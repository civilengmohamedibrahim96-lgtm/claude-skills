Ahzab reel: Higgsfield shot sheet

Make everything on higgsfield.ai in this order. When the clips are done, tell Claude "done": Claude reads your Higgsfield history, downloads the clips, and adds the narration, Alafasy recitation, verses and captions.

Step 1: credits

Your account has 10 credits on the Free plan. That is not enough for 10 clips; buy credits or a plan on Higgsfield first.

Step 2: the portrait (locks the face)

  1. Get the middle ink portrait from Kaggle: Output panel (right side) → ahzab_out/portraits/CHR-027_v2_1.png → download. If Kaggle has closed, crop the middle face from your screenshot.
  2. Higgsfield → Image → Nano Banana Pro, aspect 9:16. Upload that picture as the reference, paste the prompt below, and make 2–4 versions.
  3. Send Claude a screenshot before starting the shots.
