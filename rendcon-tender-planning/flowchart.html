Tender Planning – how it flows

How a tender email becomes a card on the board without anyone typing it, how the 4 planning engineers work from there until submission, and which tool does each step. The orange ⚙ line in each box names the tool.

Email to board

Runs automatically every hour, or when someone presses “Sync email”. Reads received and sent emails, and finds the project by reading the body – not by the subject line.
