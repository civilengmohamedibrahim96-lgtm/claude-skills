Tender Planning – how it flows

Two flows: how a tender email becomes a card on the board without anyone typing it, and how the 4 planning engineers work from there until submission.

Email to board

Runs automatically every hour, or when someone presses “Sync email”.

flowchart TD
  A(["⏱ Every hour
or Sync email button"]):::start --> B["Fetch new emails
from the tenders mailbox
Outlook · Microsoft Graph"]
  B --> C{"Read this
email before?"}:::q
  C -- "Yes" --> X1(["Skip"]):::stop
  C -- "No" --> D{"Quick check (free):
tender words or
known sender?"}:::q
  D -- "No" --> X2(["Not a tender
no AI cost"]):::stop
  D -- "Yes" --> E["🤖 AI reads the email
together with the list
of current projects"]:::ai
  E --> F["AI fills the form
type · project · client · submission date
public / private · supervisor first name
links · one-line summary · confidence"]:::ai
  F --> G{"App checks the answer
real date? project found?
confident?"}:::q
  G -- "Unsure" --> R["⚠ Needs review list"]:::review
  R --> R2{"A person accepts
or corrects it"}:::q
  R2 --> H
  G -- "OK" --> H{"What kind
of email?"}:::q
  H -- "ورد الينا – new" --> N1["New card in Unassigned
tag: New project"]
  H -- "Update / annex" --> N2["Tag: New updates
new date if given"]
  H -- "تأجيل – postponed" --> N3["Tag: Postponed
move submission date"]
  H -- "استفسارات – answers" --> N4["Tag: Queries answers"]
  H -- "Not a tender" --> X3(["Ignore"]):::stop
  N1 & N2 & N3 & N4 --> L["Save links, update-log line
and date history “from email”"]
  L --> M["Label the email in Outlook
“Tender Planning”"]
  M --> P(["Board updates for all 4 users
card shows “1 new” · notification"]):::start
