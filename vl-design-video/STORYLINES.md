# VL Design Australia — three storylines

Source: https://vldesignaustralia.com.au/ (captured into `capture/`). Every claim and image below comes from the site.

**The one thing:** You can get a professional garden design for your home without anyone visiting it.
**Viewer:** an Australian homeowner looking at a tired front yard.
**Assumptions for all three:** 25–35 s, 1920×1080 at 30 fps, no voiceover, music plus soft sound effects (free local music, or HeyGen catalog music, which is paid), brand olive `#414734` on sage `#E7E9E2`, Lato type, the real gold VIRTUAL logo.

---

## A. "The Same House" (recommended: a stranger follows one real house from before to after)

**Device / form:** before and after, as one continuous take. The site's own before and after photos of the same weatherboard house are the spine; the camera never cuts away from that yard.

- **First 2 s:** the real "before" photo fills the frame (gravel, bare fence, blue sky). The title "This yard needs a plan" lands. A thin vertical line enters from the left.
- **Beats:** hook (before photo) → tension (the camera pushes into the bare gravel) → reveal ("Online landscape design, no site visit") → proof as 4 verbs taken from How It Works: *choose* (the Front Yard package card, "From $1800") · *tell us* (the site's real "What should your new yard include?" screen) · *review* (draft) · *download* (plan, plant list, shopping list) → payoff: the line wipes across and the "after" render is revealed at full size → claim: "5,000+ landscapes designed" → end card: VIRTUAL logo, "Start a project", vldesignaustralia.com.au.
- **Look:** sage ground, olive type, the site's photos full-bleed, and one material: the before/after slider line used as the film's carrier.
- **Sound:** a warm acoustic bed, a soft swipe on the wipe, a paper rustle on the handover.

## B. "Across Every State"

**Device / form:** a flight through one world. The site's dotted Australia map is the home stage; each pin dives into a real project render.

- **First 2 s:** the dotted map of Australia fills the frame, and the Sydney pin pops with the number 32.
- **Beats:** hook (map, pins counting up: Sydney 32, Melbourne 23, Brisbane 15, Adelaide 12, Tasmania 5, Perth 4) → dive into the Gunnedah render → back out → dive into Adelaide → into Melbourne → claim "No site visit. Anywhere in Australia" → end card.
- **Look:** a white ground with the olive dot map, full-bleed renders inside circular pin reveals.
- **Sound:** an airy pad, a soft tick on each pin, a whoosh on each dive.

## C. "No Guesswork" (the bold one)

**Device / form:** type carries the argument, cut on the beat. Huge olive Lato words on sage alternate with full-bleed renders, changing every half beat.

- **First 2 s:** "No site visit." in giant type, which cuts to the backyard pool render on the beat.
- **Beats:** "No site visit" → "No guesswork" (the 3D render, from the site's "Advanced Design Tools") → "From $1800" → the numbers land (5,000+ · 98% · 30+ years) → a testimonial line ("Our Design was Perfection." — April Sawicki) → end card.
- **Look:** sage and olive colour fields, with the renders as hard cuts and no frames around them.
- **Sound:** an upbeat percussive track, cut to its beat.
